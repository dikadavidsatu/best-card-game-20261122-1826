**Ember Court — Duel of the Four Houses** is an original deck-battler for make.fun's "Make the best card game" bounty. You challenge the Ashen Regent across three duels: each turn you gain energy and draw cards, then play cards from your hand to deal damage, raise shield, heal, or draw. The twist is **Court Favor** — play two cards of the same house in a row and each gains +2 power, so every hand is a puzzle of suits, costs, and chains. The Regent telegraphs his mood in the middle of the table (defensive when hurt, bloodthirsty when you are low), so his choices can be read and countered. Win two duels to take the Court.

The game ships 48 original cards across four houses — Ember (flame), Tide (wave), Thorn (leaf), Gild (sun) — each told apart by colour *and* shape, synthesized WebAudio sound and music, deal/flip/shake/confetti animations, full mouse **and** keyboard play (1–8 plays a card, Space ends the turn, M mutes, H shows the rules), and a best-of-three match with a Regent who grows tougher every duel.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — the game plays with no network request.
